# Copperfield Depot Handbook

## Site overview

The Copperfield depot is managed by Petra Fairbanks. It has 12 loading dock doors and parking for 34 trailers.

The depot is open from 07:00 to 19:00 on weekdays. Weekend dispatch runs with a reduced crew.

## Storage

The depot has a temperature-controlled area with a capacity of 250 pallets.

Dangerous goods are not accepted unless the shipment was pre-approved by the Safety Office.

## Emergency contacts

In an emergency call the duty manager on extension 4015. The nearest muster point is the north car park.
