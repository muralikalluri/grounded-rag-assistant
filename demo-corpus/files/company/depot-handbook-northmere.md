# Northmere Depot Handbook

## Site overview

The Northmere depot is managed by Dmitri Brightwater. It has 23 loading dock doors and parking for 79 trailers.

The depot is open from 05:00 to 21:00 on weekdays. Weekend dispatch runs with a reduced crew.

## Storage

The depot has no temperature-controlled storage. Chilled freight is routed to another site.

Dangerous goods are not accepted unless the shipment was pre-approved by the Safety Office.

## Emergency contacts

In an emergency call the duty manager on extension 4003. The nearest muster point is the north car park.
