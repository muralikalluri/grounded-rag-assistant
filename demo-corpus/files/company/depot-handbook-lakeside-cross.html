Lakeside Cross Depot Handbook

Site overview

The Lakeside Cross depot is managed by Oskar Sandoval. It has 13 loading dock doors and parking for 32 trailers.

The depot is open from 05:00 to 21:00 on weekdays. Weekend dispatch runs with a reduced crew.

Storage

The depot has a temperature-controlled area with a capacity of 120 pallets.

Dangerous goods are not accepted unless the shipment was pre-approved by the Safety Office.

Emergency contacts

In an emergency call the duty manager on extension 4014. The nearest muster point is the north car park.
