Eastfold Depot Handbook

Site overview

The Eastfold depot is managed by Chiara Okafor. It has 19 loading dock doors and parking for 24 trailers.

The depot is open from 07:00 to 19:00 on weekdays. Weekend dispatch runs with a reduced crew.

Storage

The depot has a temperature-controlled area with a capacity of 120 pallets.

Dangerous goods are not accepted unless the shipment was pre-approved by the Safety Office.

Emergency contacts

In an emergency call the duty manager on extension 4002. The nearest muster point is the north car park.
