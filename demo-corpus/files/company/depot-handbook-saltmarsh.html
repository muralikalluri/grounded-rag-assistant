Saltmarsh Depot Handbook

Site overview

The Saltmarsh depot is managed by Stefan Grimsby. It has 28 loading dock doors and parking for 37 trailers.

The depot is open from 07:00 to 19:00 on weekdays. Weekend dispatch runs with a reduced crew.

Storage

The depot has a temperature-controlled area with a capacity of 120 pallets.

Dangerous goods are not accepted unless the shipment was pre-approved by the Safety Office.

Emergency contacts

In an emergency call the duty manager on extension 4018. The nearest muster point is the north car park.
